Hi there!

I am a postdoctoral researcher at the University of Geneva, in the ALGO group headed by Arnaud Casteigts. I work in theoretical computer science, with interests in graph theory and combinatorics. My current research focuses on temporal graphs, particularly questions of connectivity, exploration, and optimization.

I began my university studies at the age of 25 and obtained my PhD in Computer Science from the Gran Sasso Science Institute in 2025.

During my studies, I fell in love with mathematics, and I still enjoy exploring topics beyond my research in my spare time. When I’m not doing that, I’m probably watching a TV series :-)
